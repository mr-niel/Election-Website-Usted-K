How It Works

1
Register with your student ID, campus, department, programme, hall, and phone number
2
Upload a photo of your student ID card and verify your phone number with an OTP code
3
Once the election officer approves your ID, sign in and cast your ballot

Election Types

SRC

Campus-wide Students Representative Council elections. Every approved student on your campus can vote.

Departmental

Elections within your academic department. Only students in that department participate.

Hall

Hall of residence elections. Only students assigned to that hall can vote.

Class

Class representative elections within your department and level.
